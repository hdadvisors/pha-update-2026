**Phase:** 2 — Demand data (execution; Task 3, Session 3B)

**Corrects the entry below on the reliability NA finding, which was wrong about the cause and understated the problem.** That entry says 38 demographics rows and 151 income rows carry `NA` reliability "because their estimate is 0". That accounts for only 68 of the 189. Profiling the exports found **three unrelated causes**, and one of them is a script defect rather than a Verify-line wording problem: **20 rows have a large estimate and a `NA` MOE** — the B01001 and B01003 table totals for the four secondary counties, Charles City 6,627, Goochland 26,410, New Kent 25,105, Powhatan 31,555. ACS controls table totals to independent population estimates, so they carry no sampling error and Census publishes no MOE. The CV guard checked `estimate > 0` but never checked `moe`, so `NA / 1.645 / 26410` propagated to `NA` and **flagged the most reliable cells in the frame as unrated.** A chart filtering on reliability would have silently dropped the total population of every secondary county. The other two causes are genuine: 68 rows have a zero estimate against a positive MOE (Ashland males 80-84 is 0 ± 19, real "too small to measure" information), and 101 are suppressed S1701 subject-table cells with no estimate at all.

The same misattribution went into commit `fcd457b`'s body and the Issue [#2](https://github.com/hdadvisors/pha-update-2026/issues/2) comment. Both stand as written; this entry is the correction.

**Both halves of Jonathan's ruling applied.** The CV guard in `r/acs_demographics.R` and `r/acs_income.R` now reads: absent or zero estimate gives `NA_real_`; absent or zero MOE on a positive estimate gives CV 0, which tiers to High; everything else is `moe / 1.645 / estimate * 100` unchanged, so the grep Verify line still matches. Each script now also `stopifnot()`s that **no row is NA for any reason other than an absent or zero estimate**, so a future no-MOE condition fails the build instead of quietly dropping cells. The Verify line is amended to require a non-NA reliability only where the CV is computable, and to have the log report NAs by cause rather than as one number.
