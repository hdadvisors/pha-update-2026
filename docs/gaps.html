4  Housing gap

This section quantifies the region’s housing shortfall three ways: rental units affordable and available by income band, renters positioned to become owners, and the income required to buy at today’s prices.

4.3 Starter homes out of reach for most renters

Below the entry-level threshold: household income under 80 percent of the region’s AMI (the 0-30, 30-50, and 50-80 percent bands) — too low to reach the price a starter home requires. Straddles the threshold: the 80-100 percent AMI band, which contains the actual entry-level income threshold (about 88 percent of AMI) — some households in this band clear it, others don’t. At or above the threshold: the 100 percent and higher AMI band, which fully clears it.

  • Only about 33% of the region’s 139,683 renter households — roughly 45,408 — had income at or above the $99,400 needed to buy the region’s entry-level home over the past 12 months.
  • That leaves roughly 94,275 renter households priced out of even the least expensive quarter of homes sold regionally, concentrated in the 30,494 households below 30 percent AMI and 24,319 more in the 30-50 percent band.
  • The entry-level threshold (88 percent of the region’s 4-person area median income) falls inside the 80-100 percent AMI band, so even moderate-income renter households only partially clear it.

4.4 What renters need to buy their first home

  • Buying the region’s median-priced home ($420,000) now takes about $123,200 in household income — only 35% of area households clear that bar.
  • Even the entry-level quartile ($335,000) needs about $99,400, out of reach for more than half of the region’s households.
  • Condominiums are the most attainable entry point by structure type; single-family entry-level homes need the most income among the three types at this price tier.
  • Assumptions (preliminary): 10 percent down payment, 30-year fixed term at the current PMMS rate, 1.0 percent of value in annual property tax, and $1,500/year insurance. Not locality-specific. Actual qualifying income will vary with real per-locality tax rates, buyer down payments, and loan products.
