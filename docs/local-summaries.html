Appendix A — Local summaries

The tables below present a uniform set of key housing indicators for each of the nine localities in the Richmond regional planning area. These are preliminary — the final version will curate a subset of metrics based on local context and add narrative.

Column notes. “2022 ref.” is the 2022 annual value for PEP (population), BPS (permits), and PSH (HUD units); the 2018–2022 ACS 5-year estimate for income, tenure, home value, rent, and cost burden; and “—” where no comparable prior figure is available. “2024/25” is the 2025 value for PEP and the 2024 value for all other sources. “Change” is percent change for counts and dollar values; percentage-point difference for rates and shares.
